Operating System Principle & Lab Observation:

Why did 3 and 4 workers not give further speedup?
In our task workload (T1=2, T2=4, T3=1), the total work is 7 units. The longest task (T2 = 4 units / 400ms) cannot be split across workers. Therefore, 400ms is the hard theoretical minimum completion time regardless of whether we have 2, 4, or 100 workers! Extra workers remain idle during the remaining execution, causing utilization to fall from 83.8% (2 workers) down to 41.9% (4 workers).
